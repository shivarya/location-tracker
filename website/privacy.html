Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Location & Speed Tracker ("SpeedTrack", "the app") handles your information.

Developer: Shivarya Website: https://shivarya.dev Contact: contact@shivarya.dev

In short: SpeedTrack has no account and no server of its own. Everything it records stays on your phone unless you export it, back it up, or turn on Health Connect sync. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information the app uses

All of the following is processed and stored only on your device.

Location

  • GPS / GNSS position (latitude, longitude), altitude, speed, heading, accuracy and timestamps.
  • Satellite status (number of satellites, signal strength) for the GPS doctor.
  • Used to show live speed, record sessions and routes, and compute statistics.
  • Location is used only while you use the app or while a recording you started is running (shown by an ongoing notification). The app does not request background location access.

Motion and environment sensors

  • Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer (compass), barometer, and — where your phone has them — light, temperature and humidity sensors. Used for the G-force meter, lean angle, compass, barometric altitude and the Sensors screen, and recorded with sessions.

Physical activity

  • Step count, cadence and the activity Google Play services detects (walking, running, cycling, in a vehicle). Requires the "Physical activity" permission, which is optional.

Heart rate

  • From a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor you pair, or read from Health Connect (see below). Optional.

Profile

  • Weight, height, year of birth, sex and heart-rate settings you choose to enter, used only to estimate calories and heart-rate zones.

What leaves your device, and when

SpeedTrack itself never sends your recorded data anywhere. Some features use third-party services as part of their normal operation:

Health Connect

If you turn on Health Connect, SpeedTrack uses the Health Connect permissions you grant only to:

  • Write the workouts you record in SpeedTrack, and
  • Read heart-rate data for the time of your SpeedTrack sessions, to show it with those sessions.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for anything other than these features. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect settings. SpeedTrack's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention and deletion

  • Sessions and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Data stays until you delete it: delete a session (with undo), delete all sessions in Settings, or uninstall the app. Data you exported, backed up or wrote to Health Connect is managed where you put it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
